Questions

Before you book

Something else on your mind? Write to info@workzenly.com.

Who is Workzenly for?

US businesses with hourly or salaried staff, from a single coffee shop to a company with several locations. The employer gets the admin tools and every employee gets their own app.

Does every employee need a login?

Each person gets their own account for pay stubs, PTO and shifts. For sites where phones are not allowed on the floor, a shared tablet at the door handles clocking in.

Can it run our payroll?

Yes. Approved hours, overtime, PTO and expenses go straight into payroll, and pay stubs are published to each employee’s app on payday. Ask us on the demo call who handles tax deposits and filings for your setup.

Does it work on a phone?

The employee side is built for phones first. Managers can approve, build schedules and check who is in from a phone too; payroll is easiest on a laptop.

We already use spreadsheets. Can we move our data?

Send us your employee list and PTO balances as a spreadsheet and we will import them during setup, so nobody starts from zero.

How long does setup take?

Most small teams are clocking in within a week. We walk you through it on the demo call and stay with you for the first pay run.

Who can see salaries and personal details?

You decide. Employees see only their own record, managers see their team’s shifts and time off, and pay is visible only to the people you give payroll access to.

What does Workzenly cost?

Plans start at $29 a month plus $4 per active employee, with no setup fee and 20% off annual billing. The full plan comparison is on the pricing page.
