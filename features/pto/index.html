Why time off tracking breaks in spreadsheets

At most small businesses, a PTO request starts as a text message, gets approved in a hallway, is written on a wall calendar and is typed into a spreadsheet when someone remembers. The balance in that spreadsheet is the one nobody trusts.

That causes three problems. Managers approve a Friday off without seeing that two other people on the same shift are already out. Employees ask the owner how much PTO they have left, and the answer takes a day. And payroll learns about the day off from an email, if it learns at all, so PTO is paid twice or not at all.

PTO tracking software fixes this by keeping the request, the approval, the balance, the schedule and the pay run in one record. In Workzenly, a day off is approved once and every other part of the system knows about it.

What US law asks of your PTO tracking

Leave rules come from federal, state and city law, and they change. These are the basics as of November 2026, from official sources:

  • No federal paid vacation. The U.S. Department of Labor says the FLSA does not require payment for time not worked, such as vacations, sick leave or holidays. Those benefits are an agreement between employer and employee.
  • State paid sick leave. Many states and cities require it. In California, employees earn at least 1 hour for every 30 hours worked, and employers must allow them to use at least 40 hours or 5 days a year. In New York, employers with 5 to 99 employees must provide up to 40 hours of paid sick leave a year, and those with 100 or more up to 56 hours.
  • Vacation payout at termination. In California, earned and unused vacation must be paid at the final rate of pay when employment ends for any reason. A use-it-or-lose-it policy is not allowed there, though a cap on accrual is. Other states follow the employer's written policy, so put yours in the employee handbook.
  • FMLA. Private employers with 50 or more employees in 20 or more workweeks must give eligible employees up to 12 workweeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a 12-month period. Our FMLA leave guide covers eligibility.
  • Records. Under the FLSA, payroll records, which include PTO paid, must be kept for at least three years.

Bereavement and jury duty leave also vary by state. See our guides to bereavement leave and jury duty pay. Whatever leave management software you use, an accurate, dated balance for every employee is what lets you follow these rules and prove you did.

A buying checklist for leave management software

Take this list to any demo. Ask the vendor to show each one on screen.

  • Employee request flow. Can someone ask for a day off from a phone in under a minute, and see their balance first?
  • Coverage view. Does the manager see who else is out that day, and the schedule, before approving?
  • Accrual rules. Ask to see the accrual method you use today, such as hours per hours worked or a set amount each pay period, plus caps and carryover.
  • Separate leave types. If your state requires paid sick leave, can it be tracked apart from vacation?
  • Payroll connection. Does approved PTO reach payroll without a CSV or an email? This is where most errors come from.
  • Termination balances. Can you see an exact balance on an employee's last day for a final paycheck?
  • Importing balances. Who moves your current balances in, and how will you check them?
  • Hourly and salaried staff. Does it work for people who clock in and for office staff who never do?
  • Pricing. Check current pricing and what is included on each vendor's own site.

Questions

How do employees request time off?

From the Workzenly app on their phone, in two taps. They pick the dates, see their PTO balance before and after the request, add a note if they want, and send it. They get the answer in the same app, so nobody has to chase a manager by text.

Do PTO balances update on their own?

Yes. Balances accrue and update themselves, and approved hours come off the balance as soon as the request is approved. Employees see the same number their manager and payroll see, which ends most of the "how much PTO do I have left" questions.

Are employers required to offer paid vacation?

Not under federal law. The Fair Labor Standards Act does not require payment for time not worked, such as vacations, sick leave or holidays. Many states and some cities do require paid sick leave, and some states regulate how earned vacation is paid out, so check your state labor department.

Do we have to pay out unused PTO when someone leaves?

It depends on the state and your written policy. California treats earned vacation as wages and requires it to be paid at the final rate of pay when employment ends. Other states follow the employer's policy. An accurate balance on the last day is what makes either rule easy to follow.

Can we bring PTO balances over from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Send us your employee list and current PTO balances as a spreadsheet and we import them during setup, so nobody starts from zero. It is worth checking each balance with the employee once it is imported, because spreadsheet errors tend to carry over.

Does PTO count toward overtime?

Not under federal law. Overtime is based on hours actually worked, so PTO, holidays and sick hours do not push someone over 40 hours in a workweek. An employer can choose to count them by policy, because that pays more than the law requires.
