Why small business payroll goes wrong before payday

Most payroll mistakes at a small business are not math mistakes. They are copying mistakes. Hours live in a time clock or on paper cards, PTO approvals live in a text thread, and a receipt for oat milk sits in someone's glovebox. On the Monday before payday, the owner or bookkeeper types all of it into a payroll service by hand.

Each of those hand-offs is a place where 6 hours of overtime turns into 6 regular hours, a day of PTO is paid twice, or an $86.00 reimbursement waits another two weeks. Employees notice, and then they text you on your day off to ask why their check is short.

Good payroll software for small business closes those gaps by starting from the record that already exists. In Workzenly, the shift someone clocked in for, the overtime it produced and the PTO their manager approved are the same data that payroll reads. Nobody types it twice, and every number on a pay stub traces back to a shift someone actually worked.

What Workzenly payroll does

  • Hours that are already approved

    Managers approve a whole week of timesheets in one tap. Payroll picks up the approved hours from the employee time clock, so you review totals instead of re-entering them.

  • Overtime worked out from punches

    Breaks, overtime and late arrivals are calculated from real clock-in and clock-out times. Hours over 40 in a workweek arrive in the pay run as overtime, not as a note to fix later.

  • PTO that lands as PTO

    When a manager approves a day off, the hours come off the employee's balance and show up in the next payroll as PTO. Read more on PTO tracking.

  • Expenses paid with the paycheck

    Employees photograph a receipt, pick a category and send it. Approved claims are paid with the next paycheck as a reimbursement line, separate from wages.

  • Pay stubs in the employee app

    On payday every employee gets their pay stub in the Workzenly app, next to their shifts and PTO balance. No printed stubs, no forwarding PDFs.

  • Records and access you control

    Offer letters, W-4s, I-9s, direct deposit details and pay history live in each person's record. Pay is visible only to the people you give payroll access to.

How you run payroll in Workzenly

Here is one pay period at Northfield Coffee, from first shift to payday. Emily Carter works in the kitchen at $19.00 an hour.

  1. Employees clock in. Emily clocks in from her phone or the shared tablet at the door. During the pay period of November 1 to 14 she works 78 hours: 46 in the first week and 32 in the second.
  2. Time off is approved in the app. Emily asks for Friday, November 6 off from her phone. Her manager approves it after checking who else is out, and 8 hours of PTO are recorded for that day.
  3. Managers approve timesheets. At the end of each week, the manager approves the team's timesheets in one tap. The 6 hours over 40 in week one are already marked as overtime.
  4. Expenses ride along. Emily's $86.00 receipt for a kitchen supply run was approved, so it joins the pay run as a reimbursement.
  5. You review the pay run. On a laptop, you see each employee's hours, overtime, PTO and gross pay. Emily's line reads 72 regular hours ($1,368.00), 6 overtime hours at $28.50 ($171.00), 8 PTO hours ($152.00) and $86.00 in expenses, for $1,777.00 gross.
  6. Pay stubs publish on payday. When the run is approved, each pay stub appears in the employee's app on payday. If Emily wants to understand a line, our guide on how to read a pay stub explains gross pay, deductions and year-to-date totals.

Notice that the 8 PTO hours do not count toward the 40-hour overtime threshold. Under federal law, overtime is based on hours actually worked. You can check any week by hand with our overtime calculator.

What small business payroll has to cover

Whatever tool you use, running payroll as a US employer comes with federal duties. These are the ones the IRS lists for most employers, as of November 2026:

  • Withholding. Withhold federal income tax based on each employee's Form W-4, plus the employee share of Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%). The employer pays a matching 6.2% and 1.45%. The 2026 Social Security wage base is $184,500.
  • Federal unemployment tax (FUTA). Paid by the employer only. Employees never have it withheld.
  • Deposits. Employment taxes are deposited on a monthly or semiweekly schedule, chosen from your tax liability in a lookback period.
  • Returns. Form 941 is filed quarterly, due April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31. Form 940 for FUTA is due January 31.
  • Year-end forms. Give employees their W-2 by January 31 and file W-2s with Form W-3 with the Social Security Administration by the same date.
  • Records. The FLSA requires payroll records to be kept for at least three years and time cards for two.

States add their own income tax withholding, unemployment insurance and, in some places, paid leave programs. Before you buy any payroll app, ask the vendor which of these duties it handles for you and which stay with you or your accountant. That is the single most useful question in a payroll demo, and it is a fair one to ask us too. If you are new to the process, start with our step-by-step guide on how to process payroll.

A buying checklist for a payroll app

Use this list on any demo call, ours included. A vendor should be able to show each item on screen, not just say yes.

  • Where do the hours come from? If the answer is "you import a CSV," someone will still be copying hours every pay period.
  • Is overtime calculated from punches? Ask to see a 46-hour week and, if you operate in California or another state with daily overtime, a 12-hour day.
  • Does approved PTO reach payroll on its own? It should come off the balance and appear as PTO without a separate email.
  • How are reimbursements paid? Expenses should be separate from wages on the stub, because reimbursements under an accountable plan are generally not taxable wages.
  • What do employees see? Ask for the employee's view of a pay stub on a phone, not just the admin report.
  • Who does tax deposits and filings? Get a clear list: federal, state and local deposits, Forms 941 and 940, W-2s.
  • Direct deposit payroll setup. Can new hires enter bank details themselves during onboarding?
  • Contractors. If you pay any 1099 workers, ask how they are handled. Our 1099 vs W-2 guide explains why classification matters.
  • Access controls. Can you limit pay rates to the owner and bookkeeper while managers still approve hours?
  • Setup and switching. Who imports employees and balances, and will someone help with the first pay run?
  • Pricing. Check the current price, per-employee fees and any add-on costs on each vendor's own site or in writing.

Honest trade-offs: where Workzenly fits

Workzenly fits best when most of your people are hourly or work shifts: a coffee shop, a store, a clinic, a crew on a job site or a warehouse. Questions

Can Workzenly run our payroll?

Yes. Approved hours, overtime, PTO and expense claims go straight into payroll, and each employee gets their pay stub in the Workzenly app on payday. If you need tax deposits and filings handled for you, ask us on the demo call exactly how that works for your account.

How do employees get paid by direct deposit?

New hires add their direct deposit details during onboarding, on their own phone, along with their W-4 and I-9. The details sit in their record before the first pay run, so nobody emails a voided check or bank numbers to a manager.

Does overtime get calculated automatically?

Overtime is worked out from the time clock punches, so hours over 40 in a workweek show up as overtime before you review the pay run. Federal law requires time and a half for non-exempt employees, and some states, such as California, add daily overtime rules.

Who can see pay rates and pay stubs?

You decide. Employees see only their own pay stubs and record. Managers see their team's shifts and time off. Pay rates and payroll totals are visible only to the people you give payroll access to, such as the owner or a bookkeeper.

Can we switch payroll in the middle of the year?

Yes, though the start of a quarter is simplest, because each quarterly Form 941 return covers three months. Send us your employee list and PTO balances as a spreadsheet, and we import them during setup and stay with you for the first pay run.

Can I run payroll from my phone?

Managers can approve timesheets and PTO from a phone, which is most of the work before payday. Reviewing and approving the pay run itself is easiest on a laptop, where you can see every employee's hours, overtime and totals side by side.
