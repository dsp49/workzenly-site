Quick answer

  • Payroll calendar 2027 in short: the year starts and ends on a Friday, so a biweekly Friday payroll has 27 paydays if January 1 is one of them and 26 if the first payday is January 8. A weekly Friday payroll has 53.
  • Semimonthly (24) and monthly (12) counts never change, but eight semimonthly pay dates in 2027 fall on a weekend or Federal Reserve holiday and move to the previous banking day.
  • Federal Reserve Banks close on nine days in 2027, so direct deposit does not settle on those dates. 2026 W-2s, 1099-NECs, the Q4 Form 941 and Form 940 are all due Monday, February 1, 2027.

This payroll calendar 2027 gives you every pay date for the four common schedules, ready to paste into a spreadsheet or your payroll system. Each calendar lists the pay period, the scheduled payday and the actual pay date after moving any payday that lands on a Saturday, Sunday or a day the Federal Reserve Banks are closed, because direct deposit settles only on banking days. The biweekly and weekly calendars assume Friday paydays and a Sunday to Saturday workweek, the most common setup for hourly teams; if you pay on another weekday, shift every date by the same number of days. Below the calendars: the 27-payday year, state pay frequency rules, a worked example and the 2027 tax filing dates.

Biweekly pay schedule 2027: 27 or 26 paydays

A biweekly pay schedule 2027 comes in two versions, and which one you are on depends on your Fridays in late 2026. If Friday, December 18, 2026 is a payday, your next payday is Friday, January 1, 2027 and you are on the 27-payday track. If Friday, December 25, 2026 is a payday (usually paid on Thursday, December 24 because of Christmas), your first 2027 payday is January 8 and you have 26. The pay periods below end on the Saturday six days before payday, so each check covers two full workweeks and overtime is settled on the same check. Read how many pay periods in a year for the full explanation of why Friday payers get 27 this year.

Track A: 27 paydays, first payday January 1, 2027

The only move on this track is the first one. January 1 is a Federal Reserve holiday, so the check is paid Thursday, December 31, 2026. That puts those wages on 2026 W-2s and leaves 26 pay dates inside calendar 2027, which matters for salaried staff and per-check deductions (see the 27-payday problem). Three months have three scheduled paydays: January, July and December (if you pay the January 1 check on December 31, 2026, December 2026 gets that third check instead).

Semi monthly payroll calendar 2027: 15th and last day

A semi monthly payroll calendar 2027 always has 24 paydays, but eight of them land on a weekend or a Federal Reserve holiday this year, so the actual pay date moves earlier: January 31, February 15 (Washington's Birthday), February 28, May 15, May 31 (Memorial Day), July 31, August 15 and October 31. Everything else pays on the date, including Friday, December 31, 2027, when Reserve Banks are open. Each period covers the 1st to the 15th or the 16th to month end, so periods run from 13 to 16 days and most split a workweek; our guide to how to calculate overtime covers paying the split week.

2027 bank holidays that move paydays

Direct deposit runs through the ACH network, which settles only on days the Federal Reserve Banks are open. The Federal Reserve's 2027 holiday schedule lists nine closures. Juneteenth (Saturday, June 19) and Christmas (Saturday, December 25) are not among them, because the Fed does not move a Saturday holiday to Friday; Independence Day (Sunday, July 4) is observed Monday, July 5, and the Fed is closed. The full list of observed dates for employers is in our federal holidays 2027 guide.

When a payday lands on one of these days or on a weekend, pay on the previous banking day. Moving later risks a late-wage claim under state payday laws, and employees budget around the published date. Submit the payroll file at least two banking days before the pay date; a Monday holiday means the file for a Friday payday is due Wednesday, and a Thursday holiday pushes a Friday payday's file to Tuesday. 27 pay periods 2027: the problem and two ways to handle it

The phrase "27 pay periods 2027" worries payroll managers for one reason: salaried pay was budgeted as an annual figure divided by 26. Hourly employees are simple, because 27 paydays means 27 periods of hours and total pay follows the hours. For salaried staff on the 27-payday track you have two main choices, and both need a decision before the first affected check.

  1. Pay the regular biweekly amount 27 times. Nothing changes on the pay stub. A $60,000 salary keeps paying $2,307.69 every two weeks and the employee receives $62,307.69 in the calendar year. The cost is one extra period of salaried payroll, about 3.85% (1 ÷ 26) of annual salaried wages, plus the employer Social Security, Medicare and unemployment taxes on it and any 401(k) match calculated per check. This is the simplest option for morale and for any offer letter that states pay as a biweekly amount.
  2. Divide the annual salary by 27. Each check shrinks by about 3.7% (1 ÷ 27) and the employee receives the same annual salary: $2,222.22 per check instead of $2,307.69, a difference of $85.47 per payday. Before you choose this route, check that the smaller check still meets the salary basis test for exempt employees ($684 per week under the federal rule now in effect, higher in several states), read the offer letters (a promise of "$2,307.69 biweekly" cannot be cut without agreement), and give written notice before the first smaller check; New York and California require advance written notice of pay changes.

Decide separately how the extra check handles fixed-dollar deductions. A $2,600 annual benefit premium is $100 per check across 26 paydays; in a 27-period year you can skip it on the 27th check or recalculate to $96.30 across all 27. Percentage-based withholding simply follows the wages, though high earners can reach the Social Security wage base ($192,300 for 2027) a check earlier.

One more wrinkle for Track A employers: because January 1, 2027 is paid on December 31, 2026, calendar 2027 actually holds 26 pay dates and calendar 2026 holds 27. Wages count for tax purposes in the year they are paid, so the 27th check belongs to 2026 W-2s. If you instead pay on Monday, January 4, 2027, the extra check lands in 2027. Either is fine; pick one, tell employees in writing, and pull 2026 year-end totals after the final run.

State pay frequency rules to check before you set the calendar

Federal law does not set a pay frequency. The Fair Labor Standards Act requires that wages be paid on the regular payday for the period, but the minimum frequency comes from state law. The US Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division publishes a state payday requirements table (last revised January 1, 2023) that summarizes each state, with footnotes for the exceptions. A few examples, which you should confirm with the state agency before you commit to a schedule:

  • Texas: under the Texas Payday Law, employees who are exempt from FLSA overtime must be paid at least monthly; other employees at least twice a month. If the employer does not designate paydays, they default to the 1st and 15th.
  • California: most wages must be paid at least twice during each calendar month on regular paydays set in advance. Wages earned from the 1st to the 15th are due by the 26th, and wages earned from the 16th to month end by the 10th of the next month; weekly and biweekly payrolls must pay within seven calendar days of the period end.
  • New York: manual workers are generally paid weekly, within seven days of the end of the week, unless the employer has approval from the New York State Department of Labor to pay semimonthly. Clerical and other workers must be paid at least twice a month.
  • Arizona: two or more paydays a month, not more than 16 days apart.
  • Massachusetts: hourly employees must be paid weekly or biweekly; salaried employees may be paid semimonthly, or monthly if they agree.
  • Connecticut: weekly, with longer intervals up to monthly allowed only with the labor commissioner's approval.
  • Illinois, Ohio, Georgia: at least semimonthly, with Illinois allowing monthly pay for executive, administrative and professional staff.
  • Washington and Colorado: monthly is the minimum.
  • Alabama, Florida, South Carolina: listed with no state pay frequency requirement.

Many states also require that paydays be regular and posted, and set a maximum lag between the end of the period and the payday. The six-day lag in the biweekly calendars above fits most states, but check yours. You can always pay more often than the minimum, never less. Frequently asked questions

How many pay periods are there in 2027?

It depends on the schedule. Semimonthly payroll has 24 pay periods and monthly has 12, the same as every year. Weekly Friday payroll has 53 in 2027 because the year starts and ends on a Friday. Biweekly Friday payroll has 27 if January 1 is a payday and 26 if the first payday is January 8.

Are there 27 pay periods in 2027 for biweekly payroll?

Only for employers whose Friday paydays include January 1, 2027. That track runs January 1, 15, 29 and every second Friday through December 31, 2027, which is 27 paydays. If your first 2027 payday is January 8, you have 26, ending December 24. Employers paying on Monday through Thursday have 26.

What happens when a semimonthly payday falls on a weekend in 2027?

Most employers pay on the previous banking day, because direct deposit does not settle on Saturdays, Sundays or Federal Reserve holidays and many state laws require wages on or before the regular payday. In 2027 that moves eight of the 24 paydays on a 15th and last day schedule, for example January 31 to Friday, January 29.

Which 2027 holidays affect payroll direct deposit?

The nine days Federal Reserve Banks are closed in 2027: January 1, January 18, February 15, May 31, July 5, September 6, October 11, November 11 and November 25. Juneteenth (June 19) and Christmas (December 25) fall on Saturdays, so Reserve Banks are open on Friday, June 18 and Friday, December 24 and deposits settle normally.

When are 2026 W-2s and Form 941 due in 2027?

Monday, February 1, 2027. January 31 is the legal date, but it falls on a Sunday, so 2026 Forms W-2 to employees and the SSA, 1099-NECs, the fourth-quarter 2026 Form 941 and the 2026 Form 940 are all due February 1. Later 941s are due April 30, August 2 and November 1, 2027.

Can I pay a day late when payday lands on a holiday?

Check your state first. The FLSA requires wages on the regular payday for the period, and several state payday laws set a maximum lag between the end of the pay period and the payday, so a later date may break the rule. Paying on the previous banking day is the safe practice, and it is what every calendar on this page assumes.
