Workzenly makes time clock, scheduling, PTO and payroll software, and it appears in this list as one option. We researched every product on its own website on November 7, 2026. No company paid to be included. Features and prices change, so check each vendor’s site before you buy.

A free PTO tracker should do three things: let employees request time off from their phone, let a manager approve it without a group text, and keep an accurate balance for each person. Most free plans do the first two well. The third, balances and accruals, is where they differ, and it matters most if you pay hourly staff or follow a state paid sick leave law.

We compared eight time off trackers for US small businesses. Six have a free plan or a free edition, and we say clearly which features the free plan includes and which ones need a paid plan. We checked every user limit and price on the vendor’s own site today. If you only have a few employees and a simple policy, a spreadsheet may be enough; start with our free PTO tracker templates.

Connecteam is a mobile app for deskless teams, and its Small Business plan is free for up to 10 users with every hub and feature included. That makes it the most complete free PTO tracker we found for a small crew: you get real accrual policies, not just a request form.

Time off lives in the HR and Skills hub. You can create limited, unlimited or hourly policies. Hourly policies grant time off from the hours employees clock on Connecteam’s time clock, for example one day per 150 hours worked, and you can add tenure rules so long-serving staff earn faster. Admins can set a yearly accrual cap, a carryover limit, a maximum balance and a negative-balance limit. Employees request time off and see balances on their phone, and managers approve from the app. Past 10 users, the HR and Skills hub starts at $29 a month for the first 30 users on yearly billing.

Strengths

  • Free plan includes accrual policies, balances and the time clock
  • Accrual by hours worked suits hourly staff and many state sick leave rules
  • Caps, carryover and tenure rules built in

Drawbacks

  • Free plan ends at 10 users
  • No built-in payroll, so PTO pay is entered in your payroll provider
  • Time clock and time off sit in different paid hubs once you upgrade

Homebase is scheduling and time-clock software for hourly teams, and its free Basic plan includes time-off requests for one location with up to 10 employees. It suits a single restaurant, shop or salon that builds the weekly schedule in Homebase and wants approved time off to show up there.

On the free plan, employees request time off in the mobile app, managers approve or deny with a tap, and approved time off appears on the schedule and flows into timesheets. You can set up several leave types, such as vacation, sick and personal. What the free plan does not do is track balances or calculate accruals; Homebase puts PTO and time-off controls on its Plus plan at $70 per location per month. Payroll is a separate add-on at $49 a month plus $6 per employee paid. If you need balances, compare the paid plan with Connecteam’s free accruals before deciding.

Drawbacks

How to choose a free PTO tracker

Write your PTO policy down before you pick a tool: who earns time off, how much, how it accrues, the cap, what carries over and who approves. Then check each tracker against that page, not against its feature list. Our paid time off guide walks through the common policy designs.

Checklist for a time off tracker:

  • User limit today and in a year. Free plans end at 10 users (Connecteam, Homebase, Day Off) or 30 (Sling). Know what the first paid tier costs at your next headcount.
  • Balances, not just requests. A request form and a calendar are not a PTO tracker if nobody can see what is left. Check whether balances and accruals are on the free plan.
  • Accrual by hours worked. Hourly staff usually earn PTO or sick leave per hour worked, and several state sick leave laws are written that way. The tracker needs the hours, ideally straight from the time clock.
  • State and city sick leave. Many states and cities require paid sick leave with their own rules on accrual, caps, carryover and what you can ask for as documentation. You may need a separate sick policy next to vacation. Our attendance policy guide explains how sick leave and attendance rules fit together.
  • FMLA and other protected leave. If you have 50 or more employees, FMLA leave has to be tracked separately from PTO. See our FMLA leave guide.
  • The link to payroll. Paid time off has to reach payroll at the right rate. If the tracker does not connect, decide who copies the hours over each pay period and how you check them.
  • Records. Keep time off records with your payroll records. The FLSA requires payroll records for at least three years.
  • Final paycheck. Some states require payout of earned vacation at separation. The balance on the last day needs to be right.

Run a two-week test with three real employees: one full-time, one part-time hourly and one manager. If balances still match your spreadsheet at the end, the free PTO tracker is good enough to roll out to everyone. Employees can use our time off request forms in the meantime.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free PTO tracker?

For most small US teams, Connecteam’s free plan is the most complete: up to 10 users, accrual policies, balances and a time clock. Sling covers up to 30 users for free requests but tracks balances only on a paid plan. Jibble is free for unlimited users. Pick by headcount and whether you need balances or only requests.

Can I track PTO in Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. A spreadsheet works for a handful of employees with simple, fixed yearly allowances. It gets risky once you accrue PTO by hours worked, run more than one policy, or follow a state sick leave law, because every balance depends on manual entry. Our free PTO tracker templates include accrual formulas to start with.

Are employers required to give PTO in the US?

No federal law requires paid vacation or PTO. Many states and cities do require paid sick leave, with their own accrual rates, caps and carryover rules. For example, California’s accrual method gives 1 hour per 30 hours worked, and Washington requires 1 hour per 40 hours worked. Check your state labor department.

Do free PTO trackers calculate accruals?

Some do. Connecteam’s free plan accrues by a fixed amount or by hours worked. Jibble’s free PTO tracker page describes accruals, but check its plan limits. Homebase’s free plan and Sling’s free plan handle requests but not balances or accruals, and OrangeHRM Starter keeps automated accrual for its paid edition. Test your exact policy before rolling one out.

Do I have to pay out unused PTO when someone leaves?

It depends on the state and your written policy. Federal law does not require payout. Some states, including California, treat earned vacation as wages that must be paid in the final paycheck, while others follow the employer’s policy. Whatever tracker you use, make sure it shows an accurate balance on the last day.
