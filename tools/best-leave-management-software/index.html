The best leave management software for a US employer depends on which leaves cause the work. For most small businesses it is everyday PTO and sick leave: requests, approvals, balances that accrue correctly, and making sure the time off reaches payroll. For companies with 50 or more employees, FMLA and state paid family and medical leave programs add eligibility checks, notices and benefit claims that a simple calendar cannot handle.

We compared eight leave and absence management software tools that cover both ends: HR systems with time-off modules, dedicated leave trackers, a specialist leave administration platform and payroll with built-in PTO. BambooHR is an HRIS with time-off management built into its Core plan. It suits a small or midsize company that wants leave policies, balances and the time-off calendar on the same record as employee data, documents, hiring and onboarding.

Time-off management is on the Core plan with unlimited custom policies, so you can run separate vacation, sick, state sick leave and unpaid leave policies with their own rules, and a time-off calendar shows who is out. Employees request time off and see balances in the iOS and Android apps, and managers approve from there. Companies with 25 or fewer employees pay a flat $250 a month for Core; larger companies pay from $10 per employee per month. Payroll is an add-on for US employees, and its price is not published. BambooHR does not describe itself as an FMLA case management service, so employers with complex medical or family leaves may want a leave specialist alongside it.

Strengths

  • Unlimited custom time-off policies on the base plan

Drawbacks

  • Flat $250 a month even for a small team that only needs leave tracking
  • Payroll is a separate paid add-on
  • No dedicated FMLA case workflow described on its site

Tilt is leave management for the leaves that are hard to get right: medical, parental, family care and other extended leaves that involve FMLA, state paid family and medical leave programs and short-term disability. It suits an HR team that runs several of these a year and wants employees guided through them without a binder of state rules.

Premium Support adds a dedicated leave specialist who administers leaves for you. Tilt says implementation takes about four to six weeks for companies under 500 employees.

How to choose leave management software

List every type of leave your employees took last year and how many of each: vacation days, sick days, a parental leave, a workers’ comp absence, jury duty. The mix tells you what you need. Mostly vacation and sick days points to a PTO tracker or HRIS. Several long medical or family leaves a year points to a leave administration tool or service.

Buyer’s checklist for employee leave tracking:

  • Separate policies. You will likely need at least vacation or PTO, state or city paid sick leave, holidays and unpaid leave as separate policies. Check how many policies the plan allows and whether employees can be on more than one.
  • Sick leave at the legal rate. State rules differ. California’s accrual method is 1 hour per 30 hours worked, and Washington requires 1 hour per 40 hours worked. The software should accrue from actual hours for hourly staff.
  • FMLA tracking. If you have 50 or more employees, track eligibility (12 months, 1,250 hours, 50 employees within 75 miles), the 12-workweek entitlement and intermittent leave in hours. Under DOL rules you generally must send the eligibility notice within five business days of learning the leave may qualify. Our FMLA leave guide explains the rules.
  • State paid family and medical leave. States such as California, New York, New Jersey, Washington and Massachusetts run paid family and medical leave programs, each with its own notices and claims. Specialist tools like Tilt exist for this.
  • Other protected leave. Jury duty, military leave and, in some states, bereavement and voting leave have their own rules. See our guides to jury duty pay and bereavement leave.
  • Payroll. Paid leave must reach payroll at the right rate. Either pick software that runs payroll, or confirm an integration and test it on one pay run.
  • Records and confidentiality. Keep leave records with payroll records for at least three years, and keep medical certifications separate from the general personnel file, with limited access.
  • The schedule. For shift teams, approving leave without seeing the schedule creates gaps. Look for tools that show requests next to the shifts they affect.

Before you buy, run one realistic case in the trial: a part-time hourly employee in California who requests sick time, then a full-time employee who needs a six-week medical leave. Whichever tool handles both with the least manual tracking is the best leave management software for your business today. Frequently asked questions

What is leave management software?

Leave management software tracks every kind of time away from work: vacation and PTO, sick leave, holidays, and protected leaves such as FMLA, state paid family leave, jury duty and military leave. It keeps policies and balances, routes requests for approval, shows who is out, and keeps records. Some tools also guide employees through long leaves and benefit claims.

What is the difference between PTO tracking and leave management?

PTO tracking handles short, planned time off: vacation days, sick days and balances. Leave management also covers longer and legally protected absences, such as FMLA, state paid family and medical leave, and disability, where eligibility, notices, job protection and pay from outside programs all have to be tracked. Small teams often need PTO tracking first.

Does a small business have to offer FMLA leave?

FMLA covers private employers with 50 or more employees in 20 or more workweeks in the current or previous year. Eligible employees have worked there 12 months, logged 1,250 hours in the past year and work where the employer has 50 employees within 75 miles. They can take up to 12 workweeks of job-protected leave in 12 months.

How much does leave management software cost?

On November 7, 2026, published prices ranged from free (Connecteam and Day Off for up to 10 users) to $2 per user per month for Vacation Tracker with a $50 minimum, and $2.50 per employee per month for Tilt Essentials. HR systems that include leave, such as BambooHR, start at $250 a month flat for up to 25 employees.

Can leave management software keep me compliant with state leave laws?

It helps, but it does not replace knowing the rules. Good software lets you run separate policies by state, accrue sick leave at the legal rate and keep records. Specialist tools such as Tilt add guidance for state paid leave programs. Confirm your obligations with your state labor department and update policies when laws change.
