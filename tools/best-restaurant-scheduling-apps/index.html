Restaurants are harder to schedule than most businesses. Demand swings by the hour, servers trade shifts constantly, front of house and back of house have different roles and wages, and many of your employees are under 18. Good restaurant scheduling software helps on all four: it schedules to forecast sales and a labor target, handles swaps and pickups on the phone, separates FOH and BOH positions, and warns you before a schedule breaks a rule. How to choose restaurant scheduling software

Restaurant scheduling software pays for itself when it cuts labor cost and compliance risk, not just when it replaces the paper schedule. Check these points during the trial.

  1. POS connection. Scheduling against forecast sales needs your POS data. 7shifts lists integrations with Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed, TouchBistro, Revel and Aloha, and Homebase with Toast, Square and Clover. Confirm your POS and version.
  2. Labor target. Set a target labor percentage and see it while you build the schedule, by day and by daypart, before you publish.
  3. Overtime. Non-exempt employees, including servers and cooks, earn 1.5 times their regular rate after 40 hours in a workweek. For tipped employees paid a cash wage as low as $2.13 under federal law, overtime is figured on the full minimum wage, not the cash wage. Our overtime guide walks through the math, and California overtime law adds daily overtime after 8 hours.
  4. Minors. Restaurants hire many teenagers. Under federal rules, 14- and 15-year-olds may work only 3 hours on a school day and 18 hours in a school week, between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. (9 p.m. from June 1 through Labor Day). 7shifts and HotSchedules flag minor labor issues on the schedule.
  5. Breaks. Rest breaks of 20 minutes or less must be paid under federal rules. California requires a 30-minute meal period for shifts over five hours and a 10-minute rest period per four hours, with an extra hour of pay when one is missed.
  6. Predictive scheduling. New York City requires fast food employers to post schedules 14 days ahead and pay premiums for late changes. Chicago’s Fair Workweek covers restaurants with at least 250 employees and 30 locations worldwide, and Oregon covers food service employers with 500 or more employees. Multi-unit operators in these places need fair workweek tools.
  7. Tips and payroll. Decide whether tips and payroll stay in the same system as the schedule. Fewer exports mean fewer errors; read our shift differential guide if late shifts earn a premium.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best restaurant scheduling app?

7shifts is the best restaurant scheduling app for most independent US restaurants because it is built only for restaurants, schedules against POS sales and flags predictive scheduling, minor labor and California break issues. Homebase is the best free choice for a single small restaurant, and HotSchedules suits multi-unit groups with five or more locations.

Is there a free restaurant scheduling app?

Yes. 7shifts is free for one restaurant with up to 15 employees, Homebase for one location with up to 10, and Sling for up to 30 users. Free plans cover building and sharing the schedule and time-off requests. Time clocks, labor cost tools and tip management usually need a paid plan.

How much does restaurant scheduling software cost?

7shifts paid plans start at $39.99 per location per month for teams up to 30, with Pro at $89.99 for 31 or more employees. Homebase starts at $30 per location. Sling and When I Work charge per user, from $1.70 and $2.50 a month. Square Shifts comes with Square Plus at $49 per location. HotSchedules prices by quote. Tip and payroll modules usually cost extra.

What is a server scheduling app?

A server scheduling app lets restaurant managers publish shifts by position, such as server, bartender, host or line cook, and lets staff swap shifts, pick up open shifts and set availability on their phones. Restaurant-focused apps also link to the POS to schedule against forecast sales and track labor percentage during service.

Do restaurant scheduling apps handle tip pooling?

Some do. 7shifts includes Tip Management on its Premium plan and sells it as a $49.99 per location add-on on other plans. Homebase offers a Tip Manager add-on at $25 per location per month. General scheduling apps such as Sling and When I Work focus on schedules and time, and tips are handled in payroll or the POS.
