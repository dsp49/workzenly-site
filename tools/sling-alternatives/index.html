Workzenly makes time clock, scheduling, PTO and payroll software, and it appears in this list as one option. We researched every product on its own website on November 7, 2026. No company paid to be included. Features and prices change, so check each vendor’s site before you buy.

The best Sling alternatives for most US small businesses are Homebase if you want a free time clock, When I Work for richer scheduling rules, Connecteam for field crews and 7shifts for restaurants. Below are eight apps like Sling, with prices and plan limits checked on each vendor’s site.

Sling, now part of Toast, is employee scheduling and team messaging software. Its free plan covers shift scheduling, time-off requests, available shifts and messaging for up to 30 users. Premium costs $1.70 per user per month on annual billing ($2 monthly) and adds the time clock with geofencing, timesheets and labor cost tools. Business costs $3.40 per user ($4 monthly) and adds kiosk clock-in, attendance reports, PTO management and tasks. Paid plans have a 15-day free trial. Sling does not process payroll.

Because Sling is already inexpensive, people looking at Sling competitors usually want something it does not do: a free time clock, payroll in the same app, restaurant tip pooling, or HR tools such as onboarding. Each tool below names the buyer it fits.

Homebase is the alternative to pick if you want to clock hours without paying. On Sling, the time clock starts on Premium; Homebase’s free Basic plan includes scheduling and time tracking for one location with up to 10 employees. Clock-ins work on phones or a shared tablet, with PIN, photo and GPS options.

Homebase bills per location with unlimited employees: Essentials is $24 per location per month on annual billing and adds advanced scheduling and team messaging. It also sells payroll for $49 a month plus $6 per employee paid, which Sling does not offer. The trade-off is the free plan’s 10-employee cap, versus 30 users on Sling’s free plan.

Strengths

  • Free Basic plan with scheduling and time tracking for one location and up to 10 employees
  • Paid plans are priced per location with unlimited employees
  • Payroll add-on for $49/month plus $6 per employee paid
  • Hiring, team messaging, onboarding and HR tools in the same app

Drawbacks

  • The free plan stops at 10 employees and one location
  • PTO controls, departments and permissions start on the Plus plan ($56 per location/month billed annually)
  • Payroll, Tip Manager ($25) and Task Manager ($13) cost extra each month

When I Work is close to Sling in price and focus but offers more scheduling controls. Essentials, at $2.50 per user per month, includes auto scheduling, schedule templates, forecast tools, multi-week and multi-location scheduling, OpenShifts and shift swaps. Pro adds scheduling rules, role permissions, labor sharing and custom reporting.

Like Sling, it relies on integrations for payroll, and time tracking and attendance is an add-on you switch on per plan. There is no free plan; the 14-day trial includes everything. Choose it over Sling when you need managers at several locations to follow the same rules and share staff.

Strengths

  • Entry plan from $2.50 per user/month with unlimited users
  • Auto scheduling, schedule templates and multi-week, multi-location scheduling on Essentials
  • OpenShifts, shift swapping and in-app team messaging on every plan
  • 14-day free trial of everything, no credit card required

Drawbacks

  • No free plan
  • Time tracking and attendance is a separate add-on you switch on per plan
  • No built-in payroll; it connects to payroll providers instead

Connecteam fits teams that clock in at customer sites rather than a shop. It records GPS-verified clock-ins, geofences each work area and offers a kiosk app for shared tablets. Scheduling covers shift templates, open shifts, swaps and AI auto-scheduling that weighs availability, roles and labor laws.

Its free Small Business plan covers up to 10 users and includes a time clock, which Sling keeps on paid plans, though the free plan is limited to one time clock and one schedule. Larger teams pay per hub: Operations starts at $29 a month on annual billing for the first 30 users, plus a per-user fee beyond that. Team chat, HR documents and training are separate hubs.

Strengths

Drawbacks

  • Priced per hub, so scheduling, chat and HR each add a subscription
  • The free plan ends at 10 users
  • Paid plans charge a flat fee for the first 30 users plus a fee for each extra user

How we chose these tools

Our criteria

  • Shift scheduling for hourly employees with a mobile app.
  • Something Sling’s free or paid plans do not offer, such as a free time clock, payroll or restaurant tools.
  • A US website with a published price, or a clear note when there is none.
  • An honest payroll answer: included, add-on, or export only.
  • No ratings or paid placements; drawbacks are facts from vendor pages.

How to choose between Sling alternatives

Sling sets a low price bar, so be clear about what you are paying more for. Write down the one or two jobs Sling does not do for you today, such as clocking hours for free, running payroll or pooling tips, and judge each finalist on those first.

  • Time clock. Decide whether staff clock in on their phones (look for GPS and geofencing) or on a shared tablet kiosk. See the clock-only options in our list of free time clock apps.
  • Overtime. Federal law requires 1.5 times the regular rate for non-exempt hours over 40 in a workweek, and California adds daily overtime after 8 hours. The app should warn managers while they schedule. Our overtime guide walks through the math.
  • Breaks. Several states require meal or rest breaks. Check that break rules can be set per location.
  • Predictive scheduling. Oregon and cities such as Seattle, San Francisco, New York City, Chicago and Philadelphia have fair workweek laws for some employers. Ask how the app records when schedules are posted and changed.
  • Payroll path. If you leave Sling for payroll, choose between an app with payroll built in and a scheduler that exports clean hours to your current provider.
  • Records. The FLSA requires payroll records to be kept for three years and time cards for two. Export Sling timesheets before you close the account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Sling with a time clock?

Yes. Homebase’s free Basic plan includes scheduling and time tracking for one location with up to 10 employees. Connecteam’s free Small Business plan includes the time clock and every other feature for up to 10 users. Sling’s own free plan covers 30 users but keeps the time clock on paid plans.

Sling vs When I Work: what is the difference?

Both are per-user scheduling apps. Sling has a free plan for up to 30 users and paid plans from $1.70 per user per month on annual billing. When I Work has no free plan and starts at $2.50 per user per month, but adds forecasting, scheduling rules and labor sharing across locations.

Does Sling do payroll?

No. Sling tracks hours and labor costs and adds payroll reports on its Business plan, but it does not pay employees or file payroll taxes. You export hours to a payroll provider. Homebase, 7shifts and Deputy sell payroll add-ons, and Workzenly includes payroll with scheduling and the time clock.

Who owns Sling?

Sling is owned by Toast, the restaurant point-of-sale company, and its website now calls it Sling by Toast. It still works as a standalone scheduling app for any business with hourly shifts, not only restaurants using Toast, and it keeps its own free and paid plans.

Which Sling competitor is best for restaurants?

7shifts is built only for restaurants, with tip management, labor compliance, a manager log book and a US payroll add-on. Its free Comp plan covers one location with up to 15 employees. Homebase is another common restaurant choice, with a free plan and a Tip Manager add-on.
