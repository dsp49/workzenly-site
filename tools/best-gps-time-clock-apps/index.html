Connecteam is an employee app for deskless teams with a time clock, scheduling, forms and chat. Every punch carries a GPS stamp, starting with the free plan for up to 10 users. The paid Basic plan lists real-time clock-in and clock-out with GPS at $29 a month for the first 30 users on annual billing.

How we chose these tools

We searched for the GPS time clock apps US field businesses compare, then checked each one’s pricing page and location-tracking documentation on November 7, 2026. We gave weight to plain answers about which plan includes which location feature, because the best GPS time clock app on paper is not much use if geofencing sits on a plan you will not buy.

Our criteria

  • GPS on punches: a location stamp on every clock-in and clock-out.
  • Geofencing: whether it blocks, flags or only reminds, and which plan includes it.
  • Privacy controls: tracking limited to working time, and clear employee notice.
  • Field extras: breadcrumbs, mileage, job and cost code tracking, offline mode.
  • Cost at 10 and 30 users: including base fees and add-ons.

How to choose a GPS time clock app

Location features vary more between plans than between vendors, so decide what you need first.

  1. Pick the level of tracking. A GPS stamp records the point of each punch. A geofence time clock blocks, flags or reminds when someone is outside a set radius. Breadcrumbs record a trail during the shift. Most teams need stamps and geofences; breadcrumbs are for crews that travel between sites.
  2. Track only on the clock. Choose an app that stops collecting location when the employee clocks out. QuickBooks Time, for example, saves no off-the-clock points.
  3. Give written notice. Several states, including New York, Connecticut and Delaware, require employers to notify employees before electronic monitoring. Put your GPS policy in writing and get an acknowledgment from each employee.
  4. Count travel time correctly. Under the FLSA, travel from one job site to another during the workday is generally hours worked, while a normal commute from home to the first site usually is not. Your timesheets should show the drive between sites.
  5. Reimburse mileage where required. Federal law does not require mileage reimbursement unless unreimbursed costs push pay below minimum wage, but California and some other states require employers to reimburse necessary business expenses. A mileage log helps.
  6. Plan for bad signal. Job sites in basements, rural areas and large buildings drop service. Offline punching that syncs later prevents missing hours.
  7. Check overtime totals. Hours from several sites still add up to one workweek. The app should total them for overtime over 40, and by day in states like California. Use our time card calculator to spot-check a week.

Whichever app you choose, roll it out with one crew for a full pay period, compare the GPS timesheets with your old method, and adjust the geofence radius before adding everyone. Our guide to time and attendance tracking has a rollout checklist.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best GPS time clock app for small business?

For most small teams, Connecteam is the best value: every punch gets a GPS stamp, and $29 a month on annual billing covers the first 30 users. Timeero is better if you also track mileage, ClockShark and Workyard suit construction and field service, and BusyBusy and Jibble offer GPS time tracking for free.

Is it legal to track employees with GPS in the US?

Generally yes, for work devices and during working time, but rules vary by state. New York, Connecticut and Delaware require written notice of electronic monitoring. Limit tracking to hours on the clock, tell employees what you collect and why, and check your state’s laws before tracking personal phones.

What is the difference between GPS tracking and a geofence time clock?

GPS tracking records where the employee is when they clock in, or along a route during the shift. A geofence is a virtual boundary around a job site. A geofence time clock can block, flag or remind employees about punches made outside that boundary, depending on the app and plan.

Which GPS time clock app is free?

BusyBusy’s free plan includes GPS time tracking for unlimited users, and Jibble’s help center lists GPS and facial recognition on its free plan with no user limit. Connecteam is free for up to 10 users with GPS stamps. Geofencing and live tracking are often paid features, so check each plan.

Does a GPS time clock app drain the phone battery?

Location stamps taken only at clock-in and clock-out use very little battery. Continuous tracking, such as breadcrumbs or a live map, uses more because the phone checks its location throughout the shift. If battery is a concern, use geofences and punch stamps rather than full-shift tracking.
