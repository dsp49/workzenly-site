Workzenly makes time clock, scheduling, PTO and payroll software, and it appears in this list as one option. We researched every product on its own website on November 7, 2026. No company paid to be included. Features and prices change, so check each vendor’s site before you buy.

Before comparing Clockify alternatives, it helps to know what Clockify gives you today. Clockify is a popular timer and timesheet app from CAKE.com, used to log hours against projects and clients. As of November 7, 2026, its free plan covers up to 5 users, paid plans start at $3.99 per seat a month on annual billing, kiosk mode starts on Basic, time off and timesheet approval start on Standard, and GPS tracking and scheduling are on Pro ($7.99 per seat a month, annual). It remains a solid choice for agencies and office teams.

People look for Clockify alternatives for three main reasons: a team larger than 5 that wants to stay free, hourly staff who need a real punch clock with breaks and geofencing, or a need for invoicing or payroll that Clockify handles differently. This list covers all three. Toggl Track is the closest like-for-like timer, Jibble and TimeCamp stay free for unlimited users, Harvest is best for billing clients, and Connecteam and Buddy Punch suit shift-based hourly teams.

Toggl Track is the most direct swap for Clockify: a one-click timer, timesheets and reports by project and client, with apps for web, iOS, Android, Windows and macOS and browser extensions that sit inside the tools you already use. It is popular with freelancers, agencies and consultancies.

The free plan is for a limited number of users (Toggl’s FAQ says up to 3), so it is not the answer if Clockify’s 5-user cap is your problem. Starter costs $9 per user a month on annual billing ($12 monthly), and Premium is $16 and adds timesheet approvals. Time off approvals and accruals need a $2 add-on. Toggl Track does not record location and has no kiosk, so it is not a punch clock for hourly staff.

Strengths

  • Very quick timer with browser extensions
  • Strong project and client reporting
  • 30-day trial of paid plans
  • Apps on every desktop and phone platform

Drawbacks

  • Free plan is smaller than Clockify’s (up to 3 users)
  • Paid plans cost more per user than Clockify
  • No GPS, kiosk or shift scheduling

Jibble is a time and attendance app that is free for unlimited users, which makes it the simplest answer for a team that has outgrown Clockify’s free plan. Jibble’s help center says the free plan includes clock-in and clock-out with GPS and facial recognition, leave and public holiday tracking, payroll-ready reports, and integrations such as Slack, Microsoft Teams and QuickBooks.

Jibble also tracks time against projects and activities, and runs on phones, the web, a desktop app, a Chrome extension and a shared kiosk. Where Clockify publishes every tier, Jibble’s pricing page shows only the free plan, so ask which extras, such as more geofences or live location, are part of a paid upgrade.

How we chose these tools

We looked at the Clockify competitors that come up most often for US teams, then checked each one’s pricing and feature pages on November 7, 2026. We grouped these Clockify alternatives by why people leave Clockify, rather than ranking them on one scale.

Our criteria

  • Free plan size: how many users you can add before paying, compared with Clockify’s 5.
  • Fit for the work: project and client timers, or punch-in time clocks for hourly shifts.
  • Where key features sit: kiosk, GPS, scheduling, time off and approvals by plan.
  • Billing or payroll path: invoicing for client work, or payroll sync for employees.
  • Price per user: annual and monthly, including base fees and add-ons.

We did not include Clockify itself as an alternative, and we left out tools whose free plan or pricing we could not confirm on the vendor’s own site.

How to choose between apps like Clockify

Most Clockify vs alternative decisions come down to one question: are you tracking projects or shifts?

  • Tracking projects and clients: Toggl Track, Harvest, TimeCamp and Hubstaff keep Clockify’s project-and-client model. Compare free plan size, invoicing and approvals.
  • Tracking hourly shifts: Connecteam, Buddy Punch, Jibble and QuickBooks Time are punch clocks first. Compare kiosk options, GPS, geofencing and break tracking.

If you pay hourly, non-exempt employees, the time records carry legal weight. Check these points before you switch:

  1. Overtime by workweek. The FLSA requires 1.5 times the regular rate for hours over 40 in a fixed workweek. The app should total hours by your workweek, not just by project. See how to calculate overtime.
  2. Breaks recorded as punches. Several states require meal or rest breaks. A project timer that is simply paused does not show a break clearly; a punch clock does.
  3. Edit history. Manager changes to time entries should be logged.
  4. Record retention. Keep payroll records for three years and time cards for two, as the FLSA requires. Export your Clockify history before you close the account.
  5. Time off in the same place. If PTO requests live in another tool, balances drift. Our free timesheet templates and timesheet guide show what a complete record includes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative to Clockify?

Jibble and TimeCamp are the best free Clockify alternatives for growing teams because both offer free plans with unlimited users, while Clockify’s free plan stops at 5. Connecteam is free for up to 10 users and suits hourly shift teams. Toggl Track’s free plan is smaller, at up to 3 users.

Is Toggl Track better than Clockify?

Neither is better for everyone. Toggl Track has a polished timer and strong reports, but its free plan is smaller and its paid plans cost more per user: Starter is $9 a user a month on annual billing versus $3.99 for Clockify Basic. Clockify publishes more low-cost tiers and kiosk-only seats.

Which Clockify alternative works for hourly employees?

For hourly, non-exempt staff, choose a punch clock rather than a project timer. Connecteam, Buddy Punch, Jibble and QuickBooks Time all offer phone clock-in, a shared kiosk and timesheets for payroll. Buddy Punch’s Pro plan adds meal and rest break compliance, which helps in states with break laws.

Can I move my data from Clockify to another app?

Clockify lets you export your reports (the free plan exports to PDF), so download your full history before you switch. Most alternatives can import users and projects from a spreadsheet, but not every app imports past time entries. Keep the export, because federal law requires you to keep time cards for at least two years.

Which apps like Clockify include invoicing?

Harvest includes invoicing on every plan, including its one-seat free plan. TimeCamp adds invoicing from its Starter plan, and Hubstaff includes invoices and payments from its Grow plan. Toggl Track focuses on time and reports, and Connecteam and Buddy Punch focus on payroll exports rather than client invoices.
