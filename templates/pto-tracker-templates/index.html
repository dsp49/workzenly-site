How to use these PTO tracker templates

  1. Write down the policy. How PTO is earned (per pay period, per hour worked or a yearly grant), the cap, carryover, how far ahead to request, and what happens at termination. The trackers only apply the rules you give them.
  2. Set up one file per employee or one summary for all. Use tracker 1, 2 or 5 per person and the balance summary for the team. Keep the PTO calendar for coverage planning.
  3. Fill in the yellow cells. Accrual rate, carryover, cap and company holidays. Green cells are formulas.
  4. Update every payday. Enter approved time off and hours worked in the same week payroll runs, so the balance employees see matches their pay stub.
  5. Check flags monthly. Negative balances, people at the cap and people who have used little PTO by the fall are all easier to fix early.

Each PTO tracker Excel file also opens in Google Sheets or LibreOffice. A spreadsheet holds up for a small team with one manager. Once several managers approve requests, the risk is two people approving the same week from different copies of the file, which is when businesses look at leave management software.

US PTO rules to know

These principles are checked against the Department of Labor and the California Labor Commissioner. PTO law is mostly state and city law, so check your state labor department too.

  • Federal law does not require paid vacation. The Department of Labor says the FLSA does not require payment for time not worked, such as vacations, sick leave or federal or other holidays. These benefits are a matter of agreement between employer and employee.
  • Paid sick leave is required in many states and cities. California requires at least 40 hours or five days a year, earned at 1 hour per 30 hours worked under the standard accrual method, and lets employers cap accrual at 80 hours or 10 days. Other laws differ, so set tracker 2 to the law that covers each location.
  • Earned vacation can be wages. California’s Labor Commissioner says vacation vests as it is earned, use-it-or-lose-it policies are not allowed, a reasonable cap is, and unused vacation must be paid at the final rate of pay when employment ends. Employers can still control when vacation is taken.
  • Protected leave is separate. Track FMLA leave as its own type, even on days when paid leave covers it, so it never reduces attendance scores or gets lost in PTO totals. Our FMLA leave guide covers who is eligible.
  • Keep the records. Payroll records must be kept for at least three years under the FLSA, FMLA records for no less than three years, and California requires paid sick leave records for three years.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate PTO accrual per pay period?

Divide the yearly PTO hours by the number of pay periods. With biweekly payroll there are 26 periods, so 80 hours a year is 3.08 hours per period, 120 hours is 4.62 and 160 hours is 6.15. For accrual by hours worked, divide yearly hours by 2,080: 80 hours a year is about 0.0385 hours per hour worked.

Do employers have to offer paid time off?

Not under federal law. The Department of Labor says the FLSA does not require payment for time not worked, such as vacations, sick leave or holidays. But many states and cities require paid sick leave, and California, for example, requires at least 40 hours or five days a year, so check your state and city.

Can unused PTO expire at the end of the year?

It depends on the state. California’s Labor Commissioner says vacation vests as it is earned, so use-it-or-lose-it policies are not allowed there, although a reasonable cap on accrual is. Many other states allow expiry if the written policy says so, while a few treat earned vacation as wages the way California does. Check your state labor department before you add an expiry date to your tracker.

Do I have to pay out unused PTO when an employee leaves?

Federal law does not require it, but some states do. In California, earned and unused vacation must be paid at the final rate of pay when employment ends, for any reason. Several other states also require payout, and many leave it to your written policy. Keep balances current in your PTO tracker so the final paycheck is right.

Should I track PTO in hours or days?

Hours, if your team works shifts of different lengths or part-time schedules, because payroll pays hours and a day off can be 6 hours for one person and 10 for another. Days work well for salaried office staff with standard 8-hour days, which is why our vacation tracker counts workdays and skips company holidays.

How do I make a PTO calendar in Excel?

Put employees in rows and the days of the month in columns, and type a code such as V for vacation in each day off. Add a row that counts how many people are off each day so you can spot coverage gaps. Our team PTO calendar has 12 month tabs that update for any year and total each person’s days on a summary tab.
